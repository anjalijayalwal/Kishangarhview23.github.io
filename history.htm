History of Kishangarh

Discover the story behind the historic city.

The Beginning

Kishangarh is a historic city in Rajasthan with a strong royal heritage. The Kishangarh state was founded in the early seventeenth century by Kishan Singh, a member of the Rathore dynasty.

The city developed as a princely state and became associated with royal architecture, painting, religion, music and cultural traditions.

Royal Kishangarh

The rulers of Kishangarh supported art and culture. Their patronage helped create a distinctive artistic tradition that became especially famous for Kishangarh miniature painting.

Kishangarh Painting

The Kishangarh school of painting became famous for elegant figures, expressive eyes, delicate features and romantic themes. The best-known figure associated with this tradition is Bani Thani.

From Princely State to Modern City

Over time Kishangarh changed from a royal centre into a modern city. Today it is particularly known for its marble industry while still preserving important elements of its royal and cultural heritage.

Did You Know?

Kishangarh is widely known as the “Marble City” because of its major marble trading and processing industry.
