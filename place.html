EXPLORE KISHANGARH

Famous Places

Discover the historical, cultural and natural attractions of Kishangarh, Rajasthan.

Places That Tell a Story

Kishangarh is known for its royal heritage, beautiful architecture, religious places, lakes and its famous marble industry.

Explore some of the places that make Kishangarh a special destination in Rajasthan.

🏰

Kishangarh Fort

Kishangarh Fort is an important part of the city's royal heritage. The fort reflects the architectural traditions and history associated with the former Kishangarh princely state.

Its historic atmosphere makes it an interesting place for people interested in Rajasthan's royal past.

🏛️

Phool Mahal Palace

Phool Mahal Palace is associated with the royal heritage of Kishangarh and reflects the elegance of Rajasthan's palace architecture.

The palace is an important part of Kishangarh's cultural and architectural identity.

🌊

Gondulav Lake

Gondulav Lake is a historic water body of Kishangarh. It provides a peaceful natural setting within the historic city.

The lake is also connected with the city's heritage and traditional landscape.

🛕

Khoda Ganesh Ji Temple

Khoda Ganesh Ji Temple is a popular religious destination associated with Lord Ganesha.

Devotees visit the temple throughout the year, making it an important spiritual place around Kishangarh.
