ART & TRADITION

Kishangarh Culture

A beautiful artistic heritage of Rajasthan.

Bani Thani

Kishangarh is especially famous for its distinctive miniature painting tradition and the iconic figure known as Bani Thani.

Bani Thani is represented with an elegant face, long expressive eyes, arched eyebrows, delicate features and graceful posture.

Kishangarh Painting School

The Kishangarh school developed under royal patronage. Artists created paintings featuring devotional, romantic and courtly themes.

Music & Tradition

The royal environment of Kishangarh encouraged artistic traditions including music, poetry, painting and religious expression.

Festivals

Diwali

Celebrated with lamps, sweets, prayers and family gatherings.

Holi

A colourful festival celebrated with music, colours and sweets.

Gangaur

A major Rajasthani tradition celebrating devotion and culture.

Teej

A colourful festival associated with traditional celebrations.
