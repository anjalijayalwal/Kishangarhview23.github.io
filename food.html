How to Make

Traditional Ghewar — Simple Overview

Ingredients:

Maida, ghee, chilled water, sugar syrup and suitable toppings.

Basic process:

  1. Prepare a smooth thin batter.
  2. Heat ghee or suitable cooking oil in a deep pan.
  3. Pour the batter carefully in thin streams.
  4. Cook until the characteristic porous texture develops.
  5. Drain and finish with sugar syrup and toppings.

Traditional recipes can vary between families and regions.
