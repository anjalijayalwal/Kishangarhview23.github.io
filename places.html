Discover Kishangarh

Kishangarh is a historic city in Rajasthan known for its royal heritage, beautiful architecture, famous miniature paintings, temples, lakes and marble industry.

From historic forts and palaces to the famous white marble landscape, Kishangarh offers many interesting places to explore.

🏰

Kishangarh Fort

Kishangarh Fort is an important symbol of the royal heritage of the city. The historic fort represents the architectural traditions of the former Kishangarh princely state.

🏛️

Phool Mahal Palace

Phool Mahal Palace is associated with the royal heritage of Kishangarh. Its palace architecture reflects the elegance of Rajasthan's royal traditions.

🌊

Gondulav Lake

Gondulav Lake is a historic water body of Kishangarh. It adds a peaceful natural element to the historic landscape of the city.

🛕

Khoda Ganesh Ji Temple

Khoda Ganesh Ji Temple is a popular religious destination dedicated to Lord Ganesha. Devotees visit this temple throughout the year.

🌿

Mokham Vilas

Mokham Vilas is associated with the heritage landscape of Kishangarh and provides visitors with a quieter experience away from busy city areas.

🤍

Kishangarh Marble Market

Kishangarh is famous for its large marble market, showrooms and stone businesses. Marble is an important part of the city's modern identity.

🏔️

Marble Dumping Yard

Kishangarh's marble dumping area is known for its striking white landscape created by marble processing waste and has become a popular photography subject.

🎨

Bani Thani Art Heritage

Kishangarh is famous for its distinctive miniature painting tradition and the iconic Bani Thani figure. This artistic tradition is an important part of the city's cultural heritage.

🛍️

Kishangarh Local Markets

Local markets are a great way to experience everyday life in Kishangarh, including local food, shopping, traditional products and Rajasthan's colourful atmosphere.

🏘️

Old Kishangarh

The older parts of Kishangarh provide glimpses of traditional streets, buildings and the historic character of the city.

🛕

Temples of Kishangarh

Kishangarh and its surrounding areas have several temples connected with local religious traditions. They provide visitors with an opportunity to experience the spiritual side of Rajasthan.

🚉

Kishangarh Railway Station

Kishangarh Railway Station connects the city with other parts of Rajasthan and India and is an important transport point for visitors arriving in the city.

🪨

Marble Showrooms

Kishangarh has numerous marble and stone showrooms displaying polished slabs, tiles and decorative stone products.

📸

Marble Landscape

The large white marble landscapes around Kishangarh are one of the city's most distinctive modern sights and create unusual photographic views.

🌅

Kishangarh Sunset Views

The open landscape around Kishangarh can offer beautiful sunrise and sunset views, especially when the warm Rajasthan sky meets the white marble landscape.

🏯

Roopangarh Fort

Roopangarh Fort is a historic fort in the wider Kishangarh region. Its architecture and royal history make it an interesting destination for heritage lovers.
