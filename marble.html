Why Is Kishangarh Called the Marble City?

Kishangarh has become one of India's major centres for marble trading, processing and distribution. The city's large marble market and numerous businesses have given it the nickname “The Marble City”.

From Stone to Finished Product

01

Stone Selection

Marble blocks are selected according to their colour, pattern and quality.

02

Cutting

Large blocks are cut into slabs using specialised machinery.

03

Polishing

The surface is processed and polished to bring out its natural appearance.

04

Market

Finished marble products are displayed, traded and supplied to customers in different regions.

Marble & Local Economy

The marble sector supports a wide network of businesses, workers, transport services, showrooms and processing units. It has become an important part of Kishangarh's modern identity.

Kishangarh's Identity

Royal history and modern marble commerce together make Kishangarh a distinctive city of Rajasthan.
